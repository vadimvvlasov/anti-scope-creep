ARTICLE 6 - SERVICE LEVEL COMMITMENTS & PERFORMANCE CREDITS

6.1 System Availability Target. Synergy Software Solutions GmbH warrants that the hosted platform shall maintain a monthly Service Availability Commitment of 99.9%, calculated 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, excluding scheduled maintenance.
6.2 Scheduled Maintenance. Scheduled maintenance windows shall occur between 01:00 AM and 04:00 AM EST on Sundays. Notice shall be posted on https://www.synergy-software.de/status at least 48 hours in advance.
6.3 Service Credits. If availability falls below 99.9%, Nexus Cloud Solutions Ltd. shall be entitled to Service Credits calculated as follows:
    - Availability 99.0% to 99.8%: 10% credit of monthly fee
    - Availability 95.0% to 98.9%: 25% credit of monthly fee
    - Availability below 95.0%: 50% credit of monthly fee
6.4 Maximum SLA Cap. The total aggregate Service Credits in any single calendar month shall not exceed 100% of the monthly service fee payable by Nexus Cloud Solutions Ltd..

8.2 Automatic Renewal. Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 12-month periods unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.
8.3 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon 90 days prior written notice sent to r.vance@nexuscloud.co.uk or elena.rostova@synergy-software.de.
8.4 Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other Party: (i) commits a material breach and fails to cure such breach within 30 days after receiving written notice; or (ii) becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy.
