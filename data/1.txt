MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 15, 2025 ("Effective Date"), by and between Apex Logistics Solutions Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 100 Montgomery Street, Suite 1800, San Francisco, CA 94104 ("Client"), and DevPulse Technologies LLC, a Texas limited liability company with its principal place of business at 500 Congress Avenue, Suite 400, Austin, TX 78701 ("Developer").

1. SERVICES AND WORK ORDERS
1.1 Services. Developer shall perform software development, cloud infrastructure setup, and technical consulting services ("Services") as described in one or more Statements of Work ("SOW") executed by both parties.
1.2 Statements of Work. Each SOW shall specify the scope of work, deliverables, timelines, payment terms, and specific technical specifications. In the event of a conflict between the terms of this Agreement and any SOW, the terms of the SOW shall prevail for that specific project.

2. COMPENSATION AND PAYMENT
2.1 Fees. Client shall pay Developer the fees specified in the applicable SOW.
2.2 Invoicing. Developer shall invoice Client on a monthly basis. Invoices are due and payable within thirty (30) calendar days from the invoice date. Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate allowed by law, whichever is lower.
2.3 Expenses. Client shall reimburse Developer for reasonable, pre-approved out-of-pocket travel and hardware expenses incurred in connection with the Services within fourteen (14) days of invoice receipt.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
3.1 Work Product. Upon full payment of all applicable fees, all deliverables, custom code, architecture diagrams, and documentation created by Developer specifically for Client under this Agreement ("Work Product") shall belong exclusively to Client as a "work made for hire."
3.2 Developer Pre-Existing IP. Developer retains all ownership rights in its pre-existing tools, libraries, frameworks, and generic components ("Developer Materials"). Developer grants Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, modify, and distribute any Developer Materials incorporated into the Work Product.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
4.1 Confidential Information. Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary, financial, or technical information disclosed by the other party ("Confidential Information") and shall not use or disclose such information except as necessary to perform its obligations under this Agreement. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

5. WARRANTIES AND LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
5.1 Performance Warranty. Developer warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with prevailing industry standards.
5.2 Limitation of Liability. NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES. DEVELOPER'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED $150,000.00 USD.

6. TERM AND TERMINATION
6.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on March 15, 2025, and shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years, expiring on March 14, 2027, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section.
6.2 Termination for Convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement or any active SOW at any time without cause by providing thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.
6.3 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other party materially breaches any term and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days of receiving written notice.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of law principles. Any dispute arising hereunder shall be resolved in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.
