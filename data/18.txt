ARTICLE 6 - SERVICE LEVEL COMMITMENTS & PERFORMANCE CREDITS

6.1 System Availability Target. Aegis CyberSecurity Systems S.A. warrants that the hosted platform shall maintain a monthly Service Availability Commitment of 99.9%, calculated 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, excluding scheduled maintenance.
6.2 Scheduled Maintenance. Scheduled maintenance windows shall occur between 01:00 AM and 04:00 AM EST on Sundays. Notice shall be posted on https://www.aegiscyber.fr/status at least 48 hours in advance.
6.3 Service Credits. If availability falls below 99.9%, Horizon Media & Marketing Corp. shall be entitled to Service Credits calculated as follows:
    - Availability 99.0% to 99.8%: 10% credit of monthly fee
    - Availability 95.0% to 98.9%: 25% credit of monthly fee
    - Availability below 95.0%: 50% credit of monthly fee
6.4 Maximum SLA Cap. The total aggregate Service Credits in any single calendar month shall not exceed 100% of the monthly service fee payable by Horizon Media & Marketing Corp..

ARTICLE 8 - TERM, AUTOMATIC RENEWAL & TERMINATION RIGHTS

8.1 Initial Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date (October 15, 2026) and shall remain in full force for an initial term of 36 months (3 years) ending October 14, 2029, unless terminated earlier in accordance herewith.
8.2 Automatic Renewal. Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 12-month periods unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.
8.3 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon 90 days prior written notice sent to m.chang@horizonmarketing.io or c.dupont@aegiscyber.fr.
8.4 Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other Party: (i) commits a material breach and fails to cure such breach within 30 days after receiving written notice; or (ii) becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy.
